Privacy Notice

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its Implementing Rules and Regulations · Version … · Last updated October 12, 2026

The short version

  • We collect only what is needed to track your OJT requirements: your name, student ID, email, your OJT company list and the files you upload.
  • Your password is never stored. We keep a one-way scrambled version of it that nobody can read.
  • Only you, and the advisers and admins who review OJT requirements, can see your information. Other students cannot.
  • We do not sell your information, show ads, or use trackers. No third-party fonts, scripts or analytics are loaded.
  • Your data is stored on servers outside the Philippines (United States). Details are in section 5.
  • You can download everything we hold about you, or delete your account, at any time from Profile → Privacy & data.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The BSIT 4C OJT Checklist is a class project that helps students of BSIT 4C and their advisers keep track of On-the-Job Training (OJT) requirements. It is operated by the student developer of this project together with the advisers and coordinators who review your submissions. They decide what is collected and why, so together they are the personal information controller for this system.

Questions, requests and complaints about your data: the developer, through your OJT adviser.

2. What we collect

What Details Where it comes from
Account details Full name, student ID, email address, your role (student, adviser or admin) You, when you sign up and edit your profile
Password Stored only as a one-way bcrypt hash. We cannot see or recover your password You
OJT company list Company name, Facebook page or website, the company's email (or N/A), inside/outside Legazpi, whether you applied, and whether they replied You
Files you upload For example your resume (PDF, PNG or JPG, up to 4 MB) You
Review records Submission status, advisers' notes to you, whether the university has a Memorandum of Agreement with a company, dates Advisers and admins
Security and technical data A login session, one-time email-verification and password-reset codes (stored hashed), and the hosting provider's server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) Your device, automatically

We do not ask for government ID numbers, home address, phone number, birthdate, grades, health information or other sensitive personal information. Your resume may contain such details if you include them. Please upload only what you are comfortable sharing, and consider removing your birthdate, ID numbers and home address from it.

3. Why we use it

  • to create and secure your account, and to confirm your email address;
  • to let you submit OJT requirements and see their status;
  • to let advisers review your submissions, give feedback, and track the class's progress;
  • to email you about your account only (email verification and password reset).

We do not use your information for marketing, advertising, profiling or automated decisions, and we do not sell or rent it. Our legal basis is your consent, which you give by accepting this notice when you create your account (or when you are asked to accept it again). You may withdraw it at any time by deleting your account, though the OJT requirements for your class may then need to be reported to your adviser another way.

4. Who can see your information

  • You, always.
  • Advisers and admins of the system can see every student's name, student ID, email, OJT company list, uploaded files, statuses and notes. They need this to review and track OJT requirements. Advisers can also download class lists as spreadsheets; they are expected to keep those files private and delete them once they are no longer needed.
  • Other students cannot see your information.
  • The developer, who maintains the hosting and database accounts, can technically reach the stored data. This is limited to maintenance and to handling your privacy requests.
  • We disclose information to anyone else only when the law requires it.

5. Where it is stored and the service providers we use

We choose providers that protect data to a standard comparable to the Data Privacy Act. Nothing else is loaded from other websites: there are no analytics, advertising or social-media trackers, and even the fonts are served from this site.

6. How long we keep it (retention)

  • Account information, company lists and uploaded files are kept only while needed for the OJT requirements of your class. We will delete or anonymize them within 30 days after the OJT program for the class ends.
  • You can delete your account yourself at any time, which erases your data immediately (see section 7).
  • Email-verification codes expire after 24 hours, password-reset codes after 1 hour, and login sessions after 7 days or when you log out.
  • The hosting provider keeps routine server logs for a short period under its own policy.

7. Your rights and how to use them

As a data subject you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your data is processed (this notice);
  • Access your data and data portability: Profile → Privacy & data → Download my data gives you a copy as a JSON file;
  • Rectify wrong or outdated data: edit your name and student ID in Profile → Edit profile; edit or remove companies from your checklist;
  • Erase or block your data: Profile → Privacy & data → Delete my account removes your account, company list, uploads and review records at once;
  • Object to processing or withdraw consent: contact us, or delete your account;
  • Be indemnified for damages caused by inaccurate, false or unlawfully obtained data; and
  • Complain to the National Privacy Commission (see section 11).

If you cannot use these tools, or you want an adviser or admin to delete your data for you, contact us using the details at the end of this notice. We aim to respond within 15 days.

8. How we protect your data

  • All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Login sessions use random tokens that are stored hashed, in cookies that scripts cannot read.
  • Access is limited by role. Students can only reach their own records, and review tools are restricted to advisers and admins.
  • Uploaded files can be downloaded only by their owner and by advisers or admins, and only as downloads, never run in the browser. Only PDF, PNG and JPG files are accepted.
  • Repeated login attempts are rate-limited, and the site uses a strict content security policy against injected scripts.
  • Secret keys for the database and email are kept as private settings, never in the public code.

No online system is perfectly secure. You can help by using a strong password that you do not use elsewhere and by logging out on shared computers.

9. Cookies and browser storage

  • Session cookie (sid): keeps you logged in. It is strictly necessary and is removed when you log out or after 7 days.
  • Theme setting (theme, in your browser's local storage): remembers light or dark mode. It never leaves your device.

That is all. There are no tracking or advertising cookies.

10. If something goes wrong

If a personal data breach affects you and creates a real risk of serious harm, we will tell you and the National Privacy Commission within 72 hours of learning of it, as the law requires, and explain what happened and what you can do.
